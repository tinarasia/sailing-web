**Logbook**

- Departure: Puerto Cabrera (39.1495, 2.9305), Friday, May 23, 2025 at 12:23 UTC
- Arrival: Cala Santanyí / Cala Llombards (39.3287, 3.1475), Friday, May 23, 2025 at 19:09 UTC
- Duration: 6.8 h
- Distance: 16.7 nm
- Average speed: 2.5 kn
- Crew: Tobi, Anna, J, E, Hendrik

Slipped the lines at Puerto Cabrera and pointed the bow toward Cala Santanyí / Cala Llombards, a modest half-day's sail covering 17 nautical miles at an average of 2.5 knots. Wind 2–8 kn from the WSW, gusting to 15 kn. It backed from NE to W. Seas up to 0.6m. Wind and waves were not exactly friendly. Anchoring in Cala Santanyí was interesting, wedged between two steep cliffs about 60 meters apart. Hendrik left the boat here, and we moved on to Cala Llombards, where we spent an afternoon exploring caves by dinghy.
